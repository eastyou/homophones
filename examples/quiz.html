同音词填空练习

填空练习：根据中文提示选出正确的单词

1.Please ____ for me at the school gate.

提示：动词，表示“等待”

  • weight
  • wait

答案：wait

2.This gift is ____ you.

提示：介词，表示“为了”

  • for
  • fore
  • four

答案：for

3.She bought a ____ of new shoes.

提示：量词，表示“一双”

  • pear
  • pair

答案：pair

4.The doctor has many ____ every day.

提示：医生照顾的病人（复数）

  • patience
  • patients

答案：patients

5.The dog wagged its ____ happily.

提示：动物的尾巴

  • tale
  • tail

答案：tail

6.The wall is too ____ to climb.

提示：形容词，表示“高的”

  • hi
  • high

答案：high

7.I ____ the answer before the teacher asked.

提示：know 的过去式

  • new
  • knew

答案：knew

8.I ____ a message to him yesterday.

提示：send 的过去式

  • sent
  • cent
  • scent

答案：sent

9.What ____ is it now?

提示：名词，表示“时间”

  • time
  • thyme

答案：time

10.A ____ is flying around the flowers.

提示：会采蜜的昆虫

  • be
  • bee

答案：bee
